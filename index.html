Our Craft

Forty-eight hours in the making

Every croissant that leaves our counter began two days earlier — folded, chilled, and folded again by hand. It's slow work. It's the only way we know how.

Chef Amara spent a decade in fine-dining kitchens before opening Velvet & Crumb with one idea: bring the discipline of a Michelin pastry section to a neighbourhood bakery that anyone can walk into.

  • 48-hour fermented dough, never rushed
  • Locally sourced organic butter
  • Zero artificial preservatives, ever
— Chef Amara Osei, Founder Read our full story
