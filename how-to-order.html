How to Order

No accounts, no checkout forms, no waiting for confirmation emails. Browse, add to cart, and send us your order on WhatsApp.

Browse the Menu

Ordering steps

  1. 1

    Browse and add to cart

    Visit the menu and add anything you’d like to your cart. You can adjust quantities, remove items, and add more before you send.

    Ordering just one item? Each product also has a direct Order on WhatsApp path — no cart needed.

  2. 2

    Send your order on WhatsApp

    Click Send order on WhatsApp inside the cart. WhatsApp opens with your items, quantities, and the guide total already written out. Add your name, pickup or delivery, and any notes, then press send.

    The guide total is not final — we confirm the real total in chat.
  3. 3

    We confirm everything

    Within business hours, we’ll reply to confirm availability, the final total, and a pickup or delivery time that works for you.

    If something you ordered is sold out, we’ll suggest a similar item or a different day. Nothing is charged until you confirm.

  4. 4

    Pay and collect

    Pay by cash on pickup, or by Mobile Money before collection. Full details are sent to you in the WhatsApp chat.

    Delivery orders are paid the same way, before the courier leaves.

Ordering just one thing? Skip the cart — tap the WhatsApp button and tell us what you’d like.

Payment

Two ways to pay

Payment is always arranged in WhatsApp. This website never processes your payment.

Cash on pickup

The simplest option. Pay in person when you collect your order. Exact change appreciated but not required.

Mobile Money

Send payment before collection or delivery. Details are shared in your WhatsApp chat once your order is confirmed.

No payment on this website. We don’t take card details, hold card details, or process payments online. Everything happens either in person or through Mobile Money.
