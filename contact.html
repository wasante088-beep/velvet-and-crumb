Catering

Breakfast boxes, dessert platters, and office spreads

We cater meetings, launches, team celebrations, and events of all sizes. Everything is prepared fresh the morning of, boxed for easy serving, and delivered within the Downtown district.

  • Breakfast platters — croissants, pastries, and coffee
  • Dessert boxes — macarons, tarts, and mini cakes

Getting here

A few practical notes if it’s your first visit.

  • By foot or bike

    We’re mid-block on Maple Street, between 3rd and 4th. Bike racks are just outside the front door.

  • By car

    Metered street parking on Maple and 3rd. Two paid lots within a block. Mornings fill up quickly — come before 8 AM for easy parking.

  • By transit

    Two minutes from the Maple & 3rd bus stop. Ten minutes on foot from the Downtown Central station.
